# Free Roof Inspection: What to Expect and Why It Could Save You Thousands

Your roof protects everything underneath it — your family, your belongings, your investment. But most homeowners never look at their roof until something goes wrong. By then, a $500 repair has turned into a $15,000 replacement.

A professional roof inspection catches problems early, when they're still cheap to fix. And at ProTech Roofing, **the inspection is completely free**.

## Why You Need a Roof Inspection

Even if your roof looks fine from the ground, hidden damage can be silently destroying it:

- **Storm damage** you can't see from ground level — hail dents, lifted shingles, cracked flashing
- **Slow leaks** that haven't reached your ceiling yet but are rotting your decking
- **Ventilation problems** causing your energy bills to spike and your shingles to age prematurely
- **Warranty issues** — damage that voids your manufacturer warranty if left unaddressed

The Insurance Information Institute recommends a professional roof inspection **at least once per year** and after every major storm.

## What Happens During a ProTech Roof Inspection

### Step 1: Exterior Roof Assessment

Our certified inspector examines your roof from multiple angles, looking for:

- Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
- Granule loss (check your gutters — granules collect there)
- Damaged or rusted flashing around chimneys, vents, and skylights
- Sagging or uneven roof lines
- Moss, algae, or debris buildup
- Gutter and downspout condition

### Step 2: Attic and Interior Check

If accessible, we inspect the attic for:

- Water stains or active leaks
- Daylight showing through the roof deck
- Proper ventilation and insulation
- Signs of mold or moisture damage

### Step 3: Satellite Measurement

We use satellite imagery technology to measure your roof's exact square footage, pitch, and complexity — no ladder needed for initial estimates. This gives you an accurate scope of work and cost estimate within minutes.

### Step 4: Detailed Report

After the inspection, you receive:

- A written summary of findings
- Photos of any damage or areas of concern
- An honest recommendation: repair, replace, or monitor
- A cost estimate if work is needed
- Insurance claim guidance if storm damage is identified

## When Should You Get a Roof Inspection?

- **After any major storm** — wind, hail, heavy rain, snow
- **Before buying or selling a home** — know what you're getting into
- **Once a year as routine maintenance** — spring is ideal
- **If your roof is over 15 years old** — problems accelerate with age
- **If you notice interior signs** — water stains, musty smell, higher energy bills
