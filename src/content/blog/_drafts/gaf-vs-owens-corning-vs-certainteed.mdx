# GAF vs Owens Corning vs CertainTeed: Architectural Shingle Comparison

GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed together control roughly 80% of the U.S. residential shingle market. If you're replacing a roof, you're almost certainly choosing between these three. They're not interchangeable — each brand has real strengths and real weaknesses across warranty terms, color selection, granule quality, and what the installation actually costs you.

Here's an honest breakdown of all three, written from the perspective of a contractor who installs all of them.

## What "Architectural" Actually Means

Architectural shingles (also called dimensional or laminate shingles) are two layers of asphalt mat bonded together to create a three-dimensional look that mimics wood shake or slate. They replaced traditional 3-tab shingles as the residential standard in the early 2000s because they're heavier, last longer, and hold up better in wind.

All three brands sell architectural shingles in roughly the same weight range — 220 to 250 lbs per square (a square = 100 sq ft) — but the composition, granule quality, and manufacturing tolerances differ enough to matter over a 25-30 year lifespan.

## GAF: Market Leader with the Strongest Contractor Network

GAF holds the largest share of the U.S. roofing market and manufactures more shingles than any other company. Their flagship architectural line is the **Timberline HDZ**, which replaced the older Timberline HD around 2019.

**What's good:**
- The StrikeZone nailing area on the HDZ is the widest in the industry — about 4x larger than standard. Wider nailing zones reduce installation error, which directly affects how the shingle performs in high winds.
- GAF's LayerLock technology bonds shingle tabs together at the factory, which gives the HDZ a Class F wind rating (130 mph) without requiring hand-sealing.
- The GAF Certified and Master Elite contractor programs are rigorous. Master Elite contractors — roughly the top 2% nationwide — can offer the Golden Pledge warranty, which is the most comprehensive residential shingle warranty available.

**What's not:**
- GAF's color palette, while broad, skews toward neutral earth tones. If you want distinctive color variation or a specific regional look, CertainTeed gives you more options.
- The Golden Pledge warranty costs an additional $500-$1,200 depending on roof size, on top of labor. If you're not planning to sell the house or if your roof is relatively simple, the System Plus warranty (included with Master Elite installation) covers most of what you actually need.

**Warranty summary:**
- Lifetime limited warranty on shingles (non-prorated for the first 10 years)
- System Plus: 50-year non-prorated warranty on both materials and labor when installed as a full GAF system (shingles, underlayment, starter strip, ridge cap, leak barrier)
- Golden Pledge: 25-year 100% coverage on materials and labor, transferable once

**Typical installed cost for Timberline HDZ:** $4.50-$6.50 per sq ft, depending on roof complexity, region, and tear-off requirements.

## Owens Corning: The Brand Homeowners Recognize

Owens Corning is the brand with the pink fiberglass insulation and the pink shingles — and that's not accidental. Their **Duration** series is color-coded in a distinctive SureNail Technology strip that's become a recognizable installation marker. Owens Corning's marketing reach means many homeowners request them by name, which has helped them hold a strong #2 market position.

**What's good:**
- SureNail Technology gives the Duration shingle a reinforced woven fabric nailing strip that's genuinely easier to nail accurately under field conditions. This matters when you're on a steep pitch in August.
- Duration shingles carry a Class 4 impact resistance rating (the highest available) when manufactured with TP4 granules — marketed as Duration Storm. This is relevant if you're in a hail-prone area in states like Virginia or the Carolinas, because many insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 rated products.
- Owens Corning has one of the cleaner color systems: their TruDefinition technology increases color contrast within each shingle, making the installed roof look richer at distance.

**What's not:**
- The Owens Corning preferred contractor program (Platinum Preferred) is less selective than GAF's Master Elite program. This doesn't reflect on the shingle quality, but it does mean the warranty tier attached to your installation can vary more widely between contractors.
- Duration shingles without the Storm upgrade are standard Class 3 impact-rated, which is the same as most competitors.

**Warranty summary:**
- Lifetime limited warranty on shingles
- Platinum Protection warranty: 50-year non-prorated on materials and labor when installed as a complete Owens Corning system by a Platinum Preferred contractor
- No equivalent to GAF's Golden Pledge transferable labor warranty at the same coverage level

**Typical installed cost for Duration:** $4.25-$6.25 per sq ft. Duration Storm runs about $0.30-$0.60 per sq ft higher due to the specialty granules.

## CertainTeed: Best Color Selection, Strong in the Mid-Atlantic and Northeast

CertainTeed (owned by Saint-Gobain) is the brand that architects and custom home builders reach for most often. Their **Landmark** series is the core architectural line, but the **Landmark Pro** and **Landmark Premium** are where CertainTeed separates itself — particularly in color depth and variation.

**What's good:**
- CertainTeed's color selection is the broadest of the three. They offer over 30 Landmark colors, and the granule blending — particularly in the Landmark Pro's StreakFighter algae-resistant variants — produces shingles that look genuinely distinctive on the roof.
- The SureStart Plus warranty is straightforward and well-documented. CertainTeed's warranty registration process is simpler than GAF's system-based approach.
- CertainTeed has strong regional distribution across the Mid-Atlantic, Delaware, Maryland, and Connecticut markets, which can translate to faster material lead times and lower freight costs.

**What's not:**
- CertainTeed's SELECT ShingleMaster program (the top contractor tier) is reputable, but the network is smaller than GAF's in the Southeast and mid-Atlantic. Finding a credentialed installer in some markets takes more effort.
- Landmark shingles are slightly lighter than comparable GAF and Owens Corning products — around 220-230 lbs per square versus 240-250 lbs. Over decades, heavier weight generally correlates with better durability, though this is one data point, not a verdict.

**Warranty summary:**
- Lifetime limited warranty on Landmark and above
- SureStart Plus: 50-year non-prorated on materials and labor when installed as a CertainTeed Integrity Roof System by a SELECT ShingleMaster contractor
- Transferable once, with a $100 transfer fee

**Typical installed cost for Landmark Pro:** $4.75-$6.75 per sq ft. The premium colorways in Landmark Premium run closer to $5.50-$7.50 per sq ft installed.

## How Warranties Actually Work — and Where They Fall Short

All three brands offer "lifetime" warranties, but that word is doing a lot of heavy lifting. Read the fine print on any of these documents and you'll find the same basic structure:

- **Years 1-10:** 100% replacement of defective materials, typically no labor coverage unless you've purchased a system-level warranty
- **Years 11-lifetime:** Coverage drops to a percentage of the original cost (prorated), which means a shingle that fails at year 20 might reimburse you $800 on a $12,000 roof

The system-level warranties — GAF System Plus, Owens Corning Platinum Protection, CertainTeed SureStart Plus — all require the full roofing system to be installed using matching brand accessories (underlayment, starter strips, ridge cap, ice and water shield). Mixing brands voids the system warranty, even if the shingles themselves are covered.

Labor coverage is where the brands diverge most sharply. GAF's Golden Pledge is the most explicit: 25-year, 100% coverage on labor, backed by GAF itself rather than the installing contractor. If the contractor goes out of business (which happens), GAF still honors it. Owens Corning and CertainTeed's top-tier warranties tie labor coverage more closely to the contractor relationship, which introduces some counterparty risk.

## Which Brand Should You Actually Choose?

This depends less on brand loyalty and more on three practical questions:

**1. Are you in a high-wind or high-hail area?**
If your home is in coastal Virginia, the Carolinas, or anywhere prone to hail storms, Owens Corning Duration Storm's Class 4 impact rating is worth the premium — especially if your insurer offers a discount. A 5-10% insurance premium reduction can pay for the upgrade in 3-4 years.

**2. Do you care about the warranty being ironclad?**
If you're planning to stay in the house 20+ years and want the strongest possible labor protection, GAF's Golden Pledge — installed by a Master Elite contractor — is the most defensible warranty in the industry. It's not cheap, but it's backed by the manufacturer, not just a local contractor.

**3. Is curb appeal a priority?**
CertainTeed's color depth and variety give you the most options if you're building a custom home, doing a significant renovation, or want the roof to be a visible design element. Their Landmark Premium line in particular looks markedly richer on the roof than entry-level architectural shingles from any brand.

For most standard residential replacements — a 1,500-2,500 sq ft home, moderate pitch, no unusual wind or hail exposure — GAF Timberline HDZ installed under a System Plus warranty is the straightforward choice. It's the easiest system to warranty correctly, the widest contractor network ensures competitive pricing, and the product performs consistently.

## What Installation Quality Does to Shingle Performance

The brand matters, but it matters less than how the shingles are installed. A GAF Timberline HDZ nailed too high (above the nailing strip) will lose tabs in a 60 mph wind event regardless of its 130 mph rating. CertainTeed shingles installed over a wet deck will fail in five years, no warranty will cover it, and no manufacturer will tell you otherwise.

Manufacturer warranties explicitly exclude damage caused by improper installation, and "improper" includes common shortcuts: skipping drip edge (required by building code in most states), using fewer nails than specified, or skipping ice and water shield in zones where code mandates it. A [licensed roofing contractor](/services/roof-replacement) who follows the manufacturer's installation guide and pulls permits where required is protecting your warranty as much as choosing the right product.
